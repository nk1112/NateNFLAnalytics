NFL Team Efficiency Map: Offensive and Defensive Identity

Project Objective

This visualization evaluates all NFL teams based on offensive and defensive efficiency to answer the question:

"What makes an NFL team successful, and how does the New England Patriots' current team profile compare to the rest of the league?"

Traditional football statistics such as total yards, points scored, and win-loss record do not always capture the true quality of a team. This model uses advanced play-by-play metrics to evaluate how efficiently teams create and prevent value on every play.

Data Source

This analysis uses NFL play-by-play data from the 2025 season. Each point on the visualization represents one NFL team and is created using a combination of offensive and defensive performance metrics.

Offensive Metrics

Offensive EPA Per Play

Expected Points Added (EPA) measures the value created by an offensive play compared to the expected outcome before the play occurred.

A positive EPA play increases a team's scoring probability, while a negative EPA play decreases it.

This metric answers: "How much value does this offense create on each play?"

Offensive Success Rate

Success rate measures how consistently an offense achieves a positive result based on down, distance, and field position.

Example:

  • A 4-yard gain on 3rd-and-3 is successful.
  • A 4-yard gain on 3rd-and-15 is unsuccessful.

This metric answers: "How consistently does an offense stay ahead of schedule?"

Explosive Play Rate

Explosive plays are defined as plays gaining 15 or more yards. These plays are important because they create significant increases in scoring opportunity and can change the outcome of games.

This metric answers: "How frequently can an offense create game-changing plays?"

Defensive Metrics

Defensive EPA Allowed

Defensive EPA measures how effectively a defense prevents opposing offenses from creating value.

Lower defensive EPA allowed indicates a stronger defense because the defense is consistently reducing opponent scoring opportunities.

Defensive Success Rate Allowed

This measures how frequently opposing offenses achieve successful plays against a defense.

Elite defenses consistently force:

  • Negative plays
  • Long-yardage situations
  • Difficult third downs

Explosive Plays Allowed

Explosive plays allowed measures how frequently a defense gives up plays of 15 or more yards.

Preventing explosive plays is one of the strongest indicators of defensive success because it forces opponents to sustain long drives.

How To Read The Visualization

Each Dot Represents An NFL Team

Teams closer together have similar statistical identities. Teams farther apart have different offensive and defensive profiles.

X-Axis: Offensive Efficiency

Moving farther right indicates stronger offensive performance. Teams on the right side generally create more EPA, produce more successful plays, and generate more explosive opportunities.

Y-Axis: Defensive Efficiency

Moving upward indicates stronger defensive performance. Teams higher on the graph generally prevent EPA, limit successful plays, and reduce explosive plays.

Football Application

This visualization helps answer important questions for the Patriots:

  • How does New England's current team identity compare to playoff-caliber teams?
  • Is the biggest opportunity improving offensive efficiency or defensive performance?
  • Which teams have similar strengths and weaknesses?
  • Which opponents require a different game plan?
