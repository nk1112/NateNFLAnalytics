NFL Team Identity Map

Objective

This visualization compares every NFL team based on offensive and defensive performance metrics. The goal is to identify each team's statistical identity and understand how the Patriots compare to the rest of the league.

Methodology

Each team is represented using advanced metrics including:

  • Offensive EPA per play
  • Defensive EPA allowed per play
  • Success rate
  • Explosive play rate
  • Rush/pass tendencies
  • Sack and pressure rates

Principal Component Analysis (PCA) reduces these multiple dimensions into two components so teams can be visualized on a single graph.

How to Read the Graph

Each point represents one NFL team. Teams that appear closer together have similar statistical profiles, while teams farther apart have different styles of play.

The axes (PC1 and PC2) represent combinations of the original statistical features. They are not individual statistics, but compressed measures of team identity.

Why This Matters

This visualization helps identify:

  • Teams with similar playing styles
  • How the Patriots compare to playoff-caliber teams
  • Potential matchup similarities for scouting purposes
  • Areas where New England's identity differs from successful teams
